Branding is an effective marketing strategy tool that has been used with frequent success in the past. Today, branding is experiencing a new popularity resulting from new, innovative applications. Although there have been instances where branding has been less than successful, marketers are beginning to find the appropriate applications in a given setting. Issues and problems concerning branding strategy today include the selection of a brand name. This fundamental issue will impact on the success of a branding strategy.

Once a name is selected, marketers have to choose the advertising strategy to support and communicate the name. Finally, keeping the brand in a strong position is a critical concern. New areas of branding include corporate, industrial, and service branding. New environments are becoming the future for marketers using branding strategy.

Branding is the art of aligning what you want people to think about your company with what people actually do think about your company. Charles Singleton

To add to the new branding areas, there are new branding techniques. These techniques include brand extensions and ingredient branding. New strategies, techniques, and arenas for branding have to be managed. The goals, objectives, and mission of any organization should be in line with the branding strategy employed.

Business planning & strategy

Branding is inextricably linked to further business planning and forming strategy. It’s essential to remember main business plan steps:

  • Identify corporate vision and mission;
  • Form goals of what you strive for;
  • Depending on goals select your strategy;
  • Develop tactics which support your strategy;
  • Create tasks you need to do following your tactics.
